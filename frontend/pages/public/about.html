About PetHub

PetHub is the clinic's online system for dog and cat owners: book grooming or a check-up, keep a profile for each pet, and read the records the clinic adds after each visit.

What you can do with PetHub

Book online

Request grooming or a full check-up for a date and time that suits you.

Follow each booking

Bookings stay pending until the clinic approves them; you are emailed when that happens.

Keep pet profiles

A profile for each dog or cat you bring to the clinic.

Read medical records

See what the clinic recorded after your pet's completed visits.

PetHub also has a chat assistant for general pet-care questions and help using the site. It does not diagnose and does not replace a veterinarian.
