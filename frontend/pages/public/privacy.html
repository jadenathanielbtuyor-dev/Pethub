Privacy notice

A summary of information the current PetHub system handles.

Current system summary

This page describes the application behavior reviewed on 23 October 2026. The clinic must approve its controller identity, purposes, lawful bases, retention schedule and request process before treating this as its complete privacy notice.

Who is responsible

PetHub is the online booking and records application. The public site does not identify the clinic responsible for the service by legal name or provide a privacy-specific contact. Signed-in customers can send the clinic a message from the Contact page; it goes to the clinic's administrator message list, and the clinic's reply is shown on that page and emailed to the account's address. No response time is promised.

What we collect and why

Your account

  • Full legal name and email address: to create your account, sign you in, confirm your email address, and send you messages about your account and bookings (verification and password-reset codes, and emails when a booking is approved or cancelled).
  • Password: stored only as a one-way hash, so it can be checked but not read back. It is never shown or emailed.

Your personal and contact details

  • Contact number, home address, date of birth and gender: the registration form requests these details. Gender includes a "Prefer not to say" option. The application does not state the purpose for collecting date of birth or gender; the clinic must decide whether they are needed and explain that purpose.

Your pets and visits

  • Pet profiles (name, species, breed and age), to book services for each pet.
  • Bookings (pet, service, date, time and status), so the clinic can schedule and confirm your visits.
  • Medical records that clinic staff add after a completed visit (for example weight, treatment and notes).

Other things we keep

  • Messages you send through the Contact page (subject and message, linked to your account with your name and email address) and the clinic's reply.
  • Email delivery records for appointment and reply emails (type, recipient address, time, and whether delivery succeeded), so the clinic can see and resend an email that failed.
  • An activity log of selected account and administrative actions, with dates and related record identifiers. Some entries include action details.
  • Chat assistant: chat messages and recent turns are sent to Google's Gemini service when it is configured. Some current pet-health questions may also use Google Search grounding. The app keeps chat turns in browser memory for the current session and does not save them as PetHub chat history. Do not enter personal or sensitive details.

Who can see it

  • You, when you are signed in.
  • Clinic staff with administrator accounts, to manage accounts, pets, bookings, medical records and messages. Their user lists show your name, email address, role and whether your email is verified; they do not show your date of birth, gender, contact number or address.
  • Service providers used by the application: Supabase stores application records, Brevo sends account and appointment emails, and Google Gemini processes chat requests. Google Fonts and jsDelivr receive requests from browsers loading the site. The live hosting provider is not established by the code review.

What your browser keeps

While you are signed in, session storage keeps your sign-in token and basic account details until you log out or close the browser. Local storage keeps your light or dark display choice. The application code reviewed does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

The reviewed application does not implement an automatic retention or deletion schedule for accounts, bookings, medical records or messages. The clinic must set and publish retention periods and the process for deleting or restricting records.

Your choices

  • See and correct your name and personal details from your dashboard: Your details, then Edit details.
  • Change your password with Forgot password? on the login page.
  • The application has no self-service data export or account deletion workflow. The clinic must publish how it receives, verifies and handles access, correction, deletion and other privacy requests.

Changes to this notice

This system summary was updated on 23 October 2026. The clinic should review it whenever its collection, use, sharing or retention practices change.
