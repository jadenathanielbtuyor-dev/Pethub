Privacy notice

What PetHub collects about you and your pets, why, and who can see it.

Draft for review

This notice describes how PetHub works today. The clinic still needs to review it and complete the parts marked as placeholders before it is final.

Who is responsible

PetHub is the clinic's online booking and records system. Questions about your information go to the clinic.

PlaceholderThe clinic's name and how to contact it about privacy: to be confirmed by the clinic

What we collect and why

Your account

  • Full legal name and email address: to create your account, sign you in, confirm your email address, and send you messages about your account and bookings (verification and password-reset codes, and emails when a booking is approved or cancelled).
  • Password: stored only as a one-way hash, so it can be checked but not read back. It is never shown or emailed.

Your personal and contact details

  • Contact number and home address: so the clinic can reach you about your appointments and keep its customer records.
  • Date of birth and gender: asked for when you register. PlaceholderThe clinic's reason for needing these: to be confirmed by the clinic You can always choose "Prefer not to say" for gender.

Your pets and visits

  • Pet profiles (name, species, breed and age), to book services for each pet.
  • Bookings (pet, service, date, time and status), so the clinic can schedule and confirm your visits.
  • Medical records that clinic staff add after a completed visit (for example weight, treatment and notes).

Other things we keep

  • Messages you send through the Contact page (your name, email address and message).
  • An activity log of account events such as sign-ins, registration, email verification, password resets and changes to your details, with their dates, for security and support. It does not record your passwords or the details themselves.
  • Chat assistant: questions that the assistant cannot answer from its built-in list are sent to Google's Gemini service to write a reply. Please don't type personal details into the chat. The conversation is not saved in your browser.

Who can see it

  • You, when you are signed in.
  • Clinic staff with administrator accounts, to manage accounts, pets, bookings, medical records and messages. Their user lists show your name, email address, role and whether your email is verified; they do not show your date of birth, gender, contact number or address.
  • Service providers that run PetHub: Supabase (database), Brevo (sending emails), Google Gemini (chat assistant replies), and Vercel and Railway (hosting the website and its server). They handle the information needed for their part of the service.

What your browser keeps

While you are signed in, your browser's session storage keeps your sign-in token and your basic account details (name, email address and account type) until you log out or close the browser. Local storage keeps your light or dark display choice. PetHub uses no advertising or tracking cookies. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from jsDelivr, so those services receive your browser's request.
